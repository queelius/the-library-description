## Summary

The July structure items are fixed, and Part I (Ch 1-3) and Ch 6-9 are sound, honest teaching. The revision damaged three things: the spine has dropped out of view ("There are no joints. There are only the cuts you make" appears once, Ch 2:123; "cut" is absent from Ch 4, 5, 8, 11; Ch 10 uses "carving" once); Part III has become mostly about conditional Everettian copies and is too short to pay off (about 4,800 prose words vs 8,000; Ch 14 about 800, Ch 15 about 600); and the cascade now shows mostly as caveats ("occurrence" 44 times, "Picture A" 37 times, against about 35 concept uses of "cut").

## 3. Findings

### HIGH

**H1. The spine sentence appears once, and the inversion is never tied to the postulate.** 02:123 "There are no joints. There are only the cuts you make. The totality has no privileged decomposition..." is the only occurrence; 14:37 pays off "the cut met three times" without it. Ch 2 states the postulate's substance as Part I fact; Ch 6 never says "Chapter 2's inversion is where this was first felt; here it becomes a commitment"; neither refusal section (07:126-138, A4) says what of the inversion survives refusal; the recurring cut figure (direction.md:78) and the Ch 14 figure (direction.md:89) are absent. Suggestion: in Ch 2, mark the inversion as the picture Ch 6 will ask the reader to commit to (keep "no computable canonical index" as Part I fact); open Ch 6's postulate section by quoting the spine back; say in Ch 7 and A4 what survives refusal; reprise at 14:37 and Ch 15; add the two-carvings figure; separate theorem from postulate in foundations.md's inversion section.

**H2. Part III is too short to carry the payoff; Ch 14 does not deliver the convergence.** Prose estimate about 7,500 / 7,500 / 4,800 per Part. Ch 14's material is pre-empted by 08:119 ("The dissolution of the self in Chapter 14 follows from this") and 11:17 (Parfit, Metzinger); what Ch 14 adds is one paragraph (14:37). Ch 15's rage beat is hedged three times (15:31). Suggestion: expand Ch 14 by about 1,000 words, making "the cut met three times" concrete with worked examples (the Ch 3 coin and Ch 2 catalog; Ch 6:61's "one world"; the self-model) plus the figure; trim 08:119 to a pointer; give Ch 15 300-500 more words so the rhythm is felt; keep 15:35 exactly.

**H3. Part III drifted from "the self as the third depth of the cut" to "conditional copies do not console".** Ch 11 has no "cut" and no "the chooser is a carving"; about 70% assumes Everett right after 09:71-73 ("None of what follows rests on Everett"; "Here, Everett is a guest"). The same turn recurs four times: 11:83, 12:47, 13:49, 13:71. Unconditioned branch talk at 12:43, 12:45. Suggestion (lore first): fix outline.md Part III; rebuild Ch 11 around "the chooser is a cut"; move most QI premise analysis to Appendix C; make the "does not console" turn once at full strength in Ch 12; let Ch 11 turn on responsibility attached to a drawn boundary and Ch 13 on "we" as a cut we make anyway (13:57 seed); condition 12:43-45.

**H4. Ch 10 states its thesis mostly as negations.** 10:23-25, 10:31, 10:81; the one positive formulation is buried at 10:79 ("the labels *system*, *world*, and *I* belong to a descriptive interface, not to $M$ itself"); 10:33-41 re-run the Born comparison (fifth appearance) and Ch 5's simplicity scoping near-verbatim (10:37 vs 05:188); the planned "the description is yours" never appears; no Notes section. Suggestion: open with a positive statement adapted from foundations.md:266-269 and the spine sentence; replace 10:33-41 with one positive cascade summary (theorem, construction, finitude, postulate, optional additions); keep the Born comparison to a cross-reference; let 10:81 say what the indifference is before what it is not.

**H5. Caveat density makes the cascade show mostly as what does not follow.** "occurrence" 44, "Picture A" 37; the occurrence caveat restated about 25 times (five times in about 600 words of Ch 7: 07:17, 07:25, 07:27, 07:49, 07:53), already in the preface (00:8); "not a unique prior" about ten times (03:179; 04:223; 04:227; 05:19; 05:186; 05:188; 05:190; 10:37; A1:17; A4:5; A5:15). The footings are named four different ways (A0:5, A5:3, 09:87, foundations.md:28). Suggestion: define "the missing occurrence measure" once at full strength in Ch 7 or 8 and refer to it by name thereafter; trim the preface clause; collapse 05:186-190 and 04:223-227; standardize five footing names in foundations.md and use them in A0, A5, 07:136, 09:87.
