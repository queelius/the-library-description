# Voice, Epistemic Calibration, and Safety Audit

## Scope and sources

This read-only lens covered Chapters 7 through 10, Appendices A through E and their opener, Notes, Resources, `lore/direction.md`, `lore/themes.md`, `lore/foundations.md`, and `lore/information_hazards.md`. It checked reader relationship, companionate tone, interpretive labeling, false consolation, information-hazard controls, and crisis-copy safety.

## Summary

The voice remains clear, direct, and companionate through difficult material. The release gate fails in two places: categorical realism revives the precise information hazard that governing lore says the held-open ontology was meant to reduce, and the dedicated Resources page ends with metaphysical and guilt-contingent crisis copy while omitting immediate-danger routing.

**Finding counts:** BLOCKING: 2 | HIGH: 2 | MEDIUM: 3 | LOW: 1

## BLOCKING

### Categorical realism reverses a documented safety mitigation

- **Locations:** `lore/foundations.md:162-170,199-214`; `lore/information_hazards.md:28-49`; `chapters/07_the_inhabited_library.tex:17,120,142`; `chapters/09_the_limits.tex:89,93`; `chapters/10_indifference_of_description.tex:98`.
- **Evidence:** Governing lore holds existence open and says the cold is no longer "the hellscapes definitely exist." Current prose calls all computable structures real, describes multiplicity as structural fact, and says hellscapes are real at coordinates.
- **Problem:** The book speaks with mathematical authority where its own foundation says only a conditional interpretation is available.
- **Impact:** The categorical language intensifies the permanence-of-suffering and quantum-continuation risks specifically named in the project's safety analysis.
- **Recommendation:** Revise lore first, then condition these passages on Picture A and preserve the possibility-versus-actuality distinction in every dark catalogue.
- **Confidence:** Very high. Independently confirmed by the consistency lens.

### The Resources ending is unsafe as release copy

- **Location:** `chapters/97_resources.tex:5-17`; related lore at `lore/information_hazards.md:94-99`.
- **Evidence:** The page offers valid services but no route for immediate physical danger. Its closing assumes "the people who love you," makes their loss the reason to stay, and returns a distressed reader to "pattern," "index," "branch," and "The Library will not notice."
- **Problem:** The appeal depends on a reader perceiving loving relationships and can amplify perceived burdensomeness. It also reintroduces the book's destabilizing metaphysics on the page addressed expressly to suicidal readers.
- **Impact:** Two individually high-risk defects combine at the manuscript's most safety-critical surface.
- **Recommendation:** Update the safety lore first. Retain the opening and verified contacts, add jurisdiction-neutral immediate-danger guidance, and close with a direct, unconditional, non-metaphysical invitation to call or text now. Seek specialist prevention-copy review before release. WHO advises emergency help or a crisis line when danger is immediate, while SAMHSA distinguishes 988 support from 911 response to immediate physical danger: [WHO guidance](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/suicide), [SAMHSA 988 FAQ](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/988/faqs).
- **Confidence:** High. The missing route and textual features are facts; individual reader response remains uncertain.

## HIGH

### Everettian branching is presented as interpretation-free confirmation

- **Locations:** `chapters/08_the_observer.tex:19-25,56,60`; `chapters/09_the_limits.tex:105,117`.
- **Evidence:** The prose says accepted physics contains multiplicity, that branching arrives with no philosophical move, that every result happened, and that best-confirmed physics already has this multiplicity. It later calls the quantum reading a guest.
- **Problem:** The rhetoric turns an explicitly interpretive landmark into confirmed ontology and overstates what decoherence establishes.
- **Impact:** The book's theorem, postulate, interpretation, and consequence boundary blurs at a high-salience bridge.
- **Recommendation:** Frame unitary branching consistently as the Everettian reading and narrow the decoherence claim to effective branch autonomy.
- **Confidence:** Very high.

### Appendix B closes the Born question more strongly than the revised body

- **Location:** `chapters/A2_born_measure.tex:13`; compare `chapters/10_indifference_of_description.tex:33-35`.
- **Evidence:** Appendix B says the Born issue is "not a gap in the physics waiting on better theory," while Chapter 10 says it is relocated, not solved, and leaves the rule's empirical and mathematical work intact.
- **Problem:** The formal appendix overrules the body's careful calibration.
- **Recommendation:** Limit relocation to the demand for view-independent privilege outside quantum theory.
- **Confidence:** High. Independently found by the structure lens.

## MEDIUM

- `chapters/09_the_limits.tex:97` tells a burdened reader to "decline to live inside" the section and declares that "the right response." Use "step away from" or "decline to dwell on," and allow the reader's response without prescribing it.
- `chapters/10_indifference_of_description.tex:104` says "the mathematics itself might be wrong" before naming a postulate and quantum reading. This blurs mathematical bedrock with interpretation; name the vulnerable commitments directly.
- `chapters/A1_bedrock.tex:17` and `chapters/A4_refusing.tex:5` make Cox's calculus uniquely noncontradictory despite the appendix's own caveat. Calibrate the claim to the stated assumptions.

## LOW

- `chapters/A2_born_measure.tex:5` says "the weight a carving deserves," assigning a normative attitude to bookkeeping. Prefer "receives" or "is assigned."
