\markboth{APPENDICES}{APPENDICES}

The chapters kept the argument at eye level: settled mathematics, stated once, in plain words, then followed wherever it led. If you wanted more, this is where it lives.

Appendix A states the four results, Bayes and Cox, Kraft, dominance, invariance, formally, with the caveats the prose was too busy to spell out. Appendix B says exactly, and no more than exactly, how the Born measure and the universal prior are the same kind of bookkeeping: the seam to \textit{Multitudes}. Appendix C lays out the anthropic assumptions the book declined to adopt (SSA, SIA, the speed prior), each an extra cut, not a forced one. Appendix D takes the postulate itself and asks what you keep if you refuse it. Appendix E answers the strongest objections, from the structure, never from authority.

None of this changes the argument you already read. It only shows the work behind it. The Notes wait at the very back, after everything else: sources and small asides, gathered by phrase, for whoever wants to keep reading.
