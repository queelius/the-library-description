\markboth{APPENDICES}{APPENDICES}

The chapters kept the argument at eye level while separating four layers: mathematical results, the fair-bit universal construction, the postulate about cuts, and optional interpretations. If you wanted the formal detail, this is where it lives.

Appendix A states Bayes, a Cox-style representation result, Kraft, the distinct quantities $M$ and $m$, dominance, convergence, and invariance with their hypotheses. Appendix B limits the comparison between Born weights and program weights. Appendix C identifies the self-location rule the framework lacks and surveys proposed additions. Appendix D asks what survives if you refuse the postulate. Appendix E answers the strongest objections.

None of this changes the argument you already read. It shows the work and the limits behind it. The Notes follow the appendices, before the remaining back matter: sources and small asides, gathered by phrase, for whoever wants to keep reading.
