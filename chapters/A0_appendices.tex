\markboth{APPENDICES}{APPENDICES}

The chapters kept the argument at eye level while keeping five footings apart: theorem (the mathematical results), construction (the fair-bit universal machine), finitude (why every usable view is local), postulate (no cut is privileged), and optional additions (the outside view read ontically, a supplier of occurrences such as Everett or an infinite cosmos, an identity rule, a self-location rule, a cosmology). If you wanted the formal detail, this is where it lives.

Appendix A states Bayes, a Cox-style representation result, Kraft, the distinct quantities $M$ and $m$, dominance, convergence, and invariance with their hypotheses. Appendix B limits the comparison between Born weights and program weights. Appendix C surveys the rules offered for the two faces of Chapter 9's center, including the one built from the book's own prior, and takes the quantum-immortality argument apart premise by premise. Appendix D asks what survives if you refuse the postulate. Appendix E answers the strongest objections.

None of this changes the argument you already read. It shows the work and the limits behind it. The Notes follow the appendices, before the remaining back matter: sources and small asides, gathered by phrase, for whoever wants to keep reading.
