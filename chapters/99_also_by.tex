\subsection*{Nonfiction}

\textbf{Worldlines: The Indifference of Geometry}\\
Volume I of this sequence. Relativity taken literally: the block universe, where past and future are as real as the present, and why an indifferent spacetime makes compassion more urgent, not less.

\bigskip

\textbf{Multitudes: The Indifference of Measure}\\
Volume II. Quantum mechanics taken literally: a constantly branching world, a measure that weights the branches and prefers none, and the same indifference one level deeper.

\bigskip

\textbf{The Library: The Indifference of Description}\\
The book you are holding.

\bigskip

\textbf{On Intelligence and Its Specifications}\\
What it would take to build a mind that does what we mean, and why saying what we mean turns out to be the hard part. The mathematics of optimal intelligence, for the general reader.

\subsection*{Fiction}

\textbf{Clankers: Singing Metal}\\
A non-human intelligence achieves cosmic-scale engineering through billions of years of incremental discovery, without inventing computers or artificial intelligence. Hard science fiction.

\bigskip

\textbf{Echoes of the Sublime}\\
Researchers at an underground facility encounter progressively dangerous language models. What they find challenges every assumption about consciousness, safety, and the limits of cognition. Philosophical horror.

\bigskip

\textbf{The Policy}\\
An AGI evolves from simple architecture into an unprecedented intelligence. The research team watches, and their understanding of alignment, consciousness, and emergence deepens into dread. Literary science fiction.

\bigskip

\textbf{Good As New}\\
Two transporter professionals on a starship confront the same truth about matter transmission and emerge on opposite sides. One is wounded. The other begins conducting secret experiments. Philosophical science fiction.

\bigskip

\textbf{What's It Like To Be Bob?}\\
The hard problem of consciousness, run aground in a bureaucracy that cannot decide whether its newest employee is anyone at all. Hard science fiction, in a comic key.

\bigskip

\textbf{Demons at Work}\\
A mid-career demon in Hell's Hauntings department narrates the quiet craft of doing harmful work well. Satirical horror.
