\markboth{ABOUT THE AUTHOR}{ABOUT THE AUTHOR}

Alex Towell is a mathematician and computer scientist. He holds two master's degrees (mathematics and computer science) from Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, where he is completing a PhD in computer science. His research is in artificial intelligence and machine learning, with Bayesian statistics underneath much of it, which is to say in prediction, and in the weighting of possibilities, which is to say in the subject of this book.

In 2020, he was diagnosed with stage 3 cancer. In 2024, it progressed to stage 4. He has spent the years since in chemotherapy, surgeries, and the particular kind of uncertainty that comes from not knowing what the next scan will show. He wrote this book in the middle of that.

He did not write it because he is dying. Everyone is dying. He wrote it because the people around him deserve to be seen, and the finite stretch where his life runs alongside theirs is the stretch he has.

He lives in Illinois with the people he loves.
