\markboth{RESOURCES}{RESOURCES}

If you may act on suicidal thoughts soon, have already begun to act, or cannot keep yourself safe, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department now. If possible, ask another person to stay with you while you do.

Tell someone directly what is happening. You can use these words: ``I am thinking about suicide and need help staying safe.'' Then call or text one of the services below.

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{0.6em}
\item \textbf{United States.} 988 Suicide \& Crisis Lifeline. Call or text \textbf{988}, any time, any day.
\item \textbf{Canada.} 9-8-8: Suicide Crisis Helpline. Call or text \textbf{9-8-8}, any time, any day.
\item \textbf{United Kingdom and Ireland.} Samaritans. Call \textbf{116 123}, free, any time.
\item \textbf{Anywhere.} \url{https://findahelpline.com} can help you find a verified local service.
\end{itemize}

\bigskip

\noindent You do not need to settle anything in this book before asking for help. Put it down, make the call or send the text, and, if you can, stay with another person, or stay on the line, while the immediate danger passes.
