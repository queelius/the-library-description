\markboth{RESOURCES}{RESOURCES}

If this book, or anything outside it, has left you in a place where you are thinking about ending your life, please reach out to one of these. Some readers will need them, and needing them is no kind of failure.

\begin{itemize}
\setlength{\itemsep}{0.6em}
\item \textbf{United States.} 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Call or text \textbf{988}, any time, any day.
\item \textbf{United States and Canada.} Crisis Text Line. Text \textbf{HOME} to \textbf{741741}.
\item \textbf{United Kingdom and Ireland.} Samaritans. Call \textbf{116 123}, free, any time.
\item \textbf{Anywhere.} \texttt{findahelpline.com} will give you a crisis line for almost any country. The International Association for Suicide Prevention keeps a directory of centers at \texttt{iasp.info/resources/Crisis\_Centres}.
\end{itemize}
