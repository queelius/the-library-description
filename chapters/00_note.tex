\chapter*{A Note Before You Begin}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\protect\numberline{}A Note Before You Begin}

Some readers will come to this book at a hard moment. A few will come to it because of an idea they have already met somewhere: that if every possible version of you exists at some index, then your life is either weightless or inescapable, and in neither case worth much. I want to be plain about that here, before any of the mathematics, rather than leave it for a chapter you might not reach.

If you came hoping this book would give you permission to stop, I want to give you the opposite. The measure does not give permission. The mathematics does not give permission. What the honest version removes is certainty, in either direction. It does not promise that some copy of you escapes, and it does not promise that you are trapped. The dark readings are confident ones, and confidence is the single thing the framework will not support. What is real is smaller, and better: one finite person, writing to another, asking you to stay.

You will read, in these pages, that the structure does not notice you, that the measure singles no one out, that your worst moments are permanent at their coordinates. All of that is in the book because it is honest. None of it is a reason to go. The book's whole argument is that the warmth missing from the structure is the warmth we make for each other, here, at the one index we can actually reach, and that this is not a consolation prize. It is the entire point.

If you are in that hard moment now, there are a few numbers and names at the back of this book, under \textit{Resources}. They are there because some readers will need them, and needing them is no kind of failure. Please use them.
